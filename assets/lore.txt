It was a time of total darkness.
Beings born of shells and rock and the magic of the earth were infesting the peaceful land. Nicknamed 'Shellers' by the local community, they ran wild, never stopping, driven by a hivemind deep below the surface.
Each of the Slime tribe were panicked; what could they do, now? They could no longer go outside to the moon, to the rich grasses, to the waters; they were drying out, and they couldn't survive much longer.
Until one surfaced from the others. His name was Bloob.
Bloob swore to shut down the Shellers and their hivemind from the source; no matter what it took. 
You are Bloob. Save them. We believe in you.

Sheller Info:
Yellow creatures with sharp scuttling legs and hard-shelled bodies. They are unable to be killed, and much maneuvering must be done to get around them. They are simple-minded, though, and only move in a set straight line. Timing is key.

Spike Info:
Sharp iron spikes built to impale Slimes. Likely set by something more intelligent than the Shellers...

Portal Info:
Shellers guard these portals with their lives. They lead further into their dwellings. These do not injure Slimes, and is the only way to get deeper.
